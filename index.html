Daily high temperature. The shaded band is your temperature preference; green dots also meet your rain limit. Gaps indicate missing readings.

About this view. Twelve selected airport stations, January 1–September 21, 2017. “Comfortable” means the daily high is within your inclusive range and precipitation is at or below your limit. Percentages exclude days missing either measurement; missing days break streaks. This is a partial-year weather comparison, not a long-term climate ranking.
Data and methods

Source: the supplied weather.csv. Temperature is interpreted as °F and precipitation as inches based on the supplied values; confirm these units with the original dataset documentation. Temperature readings outside −80 to 140°F, negative precipitation, and high temperatures below the recorded low are excluded. These are screening rules, not a full quality assessment. Stations are identified by name, state, latitude, and longitude. The charts use TMAX and PRCP; average temperature is not substituted for missing highs.
